How Direct Peer-to-Peer Works

📱 Your Browser or Phone

Open onmychat.com from anywhere. Chat with AI, browse folders, edit office documents, or stream videos.

← Encrypted WebRTC P2P →
The signaling gateway only introduces your devices — your files and chats never sit on a third-party cloud.

🏠 Your Home Computer / NAS

Runs the lightweight open-source node over your existing folders, local LLMs (Ollama / NPU), and GPU.

Everything in one private workspace

Powered by On My Disk Direct for peer-to-peer file sharing — paired with On My Chat so your local AI works right next to your files.

Share straight from your drive

Send a link to a folder, photo album, or 20 GB video right from your laptop. Friends download or stream directly from your disk without cloud upload limits.

AI that knows your documents

Point On My Chat at your PDFs, books, notes, and photos. Local semantic search (RAG) and on-device vision find answers by meaning while keeping every file at home.

Built-in Office & Media viewers

View and edit Word documents, spreadsheets, and presentations right inside the browser (WordCraft, GridCraft, DeckCraft), or have your AI draft formatted docs for you.

Remote P2P browser terminal

Open a terminal to your home machine from any browser over WebRTC — no VPN, static IP, or router setup. Drive coding agents like OpenCode, Claude Code, or Aider remotely.

Creative Studio & AI Companions

Create illustrations, visual stories, and custom AI companions on your own GPU or shared community nodes — and share your creations directly on CivitAI.

Cryptographic identity, zero accounts

No central password database to breach. Your identity is a cryptographic DID keypair generated in your browser, keeping settings and chat history encrypted end-to-end.

Start in 5 seconds — or host your own node

No lock-in. Use the web client immediately, then connect your own hardware whenever you want.
